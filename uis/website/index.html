What we offer

Outpatient services designed around you

Every service is available with same-day booking in most locations. Call us or use our online form to get started.

Primary care

Routine check-ups, vaccinations, school physicals, and same-day sick visits. No referral needed for most services.

Specialist consultations

Cardiology, dermatology, endocrinology, orthopaedics, and women's health — coordinated within our network.

Chronic disease management

Ongoing support for diabetes, hypertension, asthma, and other long-term conditions with regular monitoring.

Preventive health

Annual physicals, health screenings, immunisations, and wellness counselling to keep you healthy year-round.

Women's health

Annual exams, family planning, prenatal and postnatal care, and menopause management in a comfortable setting.

Mental health

Confidential counselling, anxiety and depression management, and behavioural health support with licensed therapists.
